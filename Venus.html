Venus - The Hottest Planet

Venus is the second planet from the Sun. It is known for its extremely hot temperatures and thick atmosphere.

Five Interesting Facts About Venus

  • Venus actually spins backward! Unlike most other planets in the solar system, Venus rotates from east to west, which means the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east.
  • Venus has a diameter of about 12,104 kilometers.
  • It has earned the nickname of Earth’s "evil twin". This is because it is very similar to Earth in size, mass, and internal structure, but its surface features crushing atmospheric pressure which is about 93 times Earth's.
  • The average surface temperature of Venus is about 465 degrees Celsius.
  • Venus takes about 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun, but about 243 Earth days to rotate once.
