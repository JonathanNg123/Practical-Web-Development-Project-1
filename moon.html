About the Moon

  • The Moon is the fifth-largest moon in our solar system and the only celestial body beyond Earth visited by humans.
  • It is tidally locked to Earth, meaning the exact same side of the Moon always faces our planet.
  • The Moon has virtually no atmosphere, which leads to extreme temperature swings and a surface heavily scarred by craters.
  • Its gravitational pull is the main force responsible for creating Earth's ocean tides.
  • The Moon is slowly moving away from Earth at a rate of about 1.5 inches (3.8 centimeters) every year.
