About the Solar System

  • Our solar system formed approximately 4.6 billion years ago from the gravitational collapse of a giant interstellar gas cloud.
  • It is anchored by the Sun and contains eight planets, numerous dwarf planets, over 200 known moons, and countless asteroids and comets.
  • All eight planets orbit the Sun in an elliptical path and travel in the same counter-clockwise direction.
  • The solar system is located in the Orion Arm of the Milky Way galaxy, taking about 230 million years to complete one galactic orbit.
  • It stretches across billions of miles, extending from the innermost planets all the way out past the Kuiper Belt.
