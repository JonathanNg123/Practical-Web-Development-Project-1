Welcome to Mercury

Facts on Mercury

  • Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and the closest to the Sun.
  • A year on Mercury is very short—it orbits the Sun in just 88 Earth days.
  • Despite being closest to the Sun, Venus is actually hotter, but Mercury has extreme temperature swings from -290°F to 800°F.
  • Mercury has a rocky, heavily cratered surface similar to Earth's Moon.
  • Mercury has zero moons and zero rings.
