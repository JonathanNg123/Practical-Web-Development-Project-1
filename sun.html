Fact on the Sun

  • The Sun is a G-type main-sequence star (yellow dwarf) located at the center of our solar system.
  • It contains about 99.8% of all the mass in the entire solar system and could fit about 1.3 million Earths inside it.
  • The surface reaches temperatures of about 10,000°F (5,500°C), while its core reaches 27 million°F (15 million°C).
  • The Sun is about 93 million miles away from Earth, and its light takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach us.
  • It goes through an active and quiet magnetic cycle approximately every 11 years, producing sunspots and solar flares.
