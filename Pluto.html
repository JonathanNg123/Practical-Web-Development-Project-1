About Pluto

  • Pluto is a dwarf planet located in the Kuiper Belt, a distant region filled with icy bodies beyond Neptune.
  • It was considered the ninth planet in our solar system until the International Astronomical Union reclassified it in 2006.
  • Pluto features a prominent heart-shaped glacier named Tombaugh Regio, rich in nitrogen, methane, and carbon monoxide ice.
  • A single year on Pluto takes about 248 Earth years to complete its massive orbit around the Sun.
  • Pluto has five known moons, with its largest moon, Charon, being so big that Pluto and Charon orbit a common center of mass outside Pluto itself.
