About Earth

  • Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known astronomical object in the universe to harbor life.
  • Approximately 71% of Earth's surface is covered in water, with oceans holding about 96.5% of all Earth's water.
  • Its atmosphere is composed of roughly 78% nitrogen and 21% oxygen, which creates the optimal conditions for living organisms.
  • Earth completes one rotation on its axis every 24 hours (a day) and orbits the Sun once every 365.25 days (a year).
  • Earth has one natural satellite, which we call the Moon.
